# Articles

Public writing on human–AI relational governance, published on LinkedIn and at [gaianexus.online](https://gaianexus.online).

These are not framework papers. They are shorter pieces working out ideas in public, often ahead of any formal treatment — and several of the frameworks in this repository began here before they had a paper behind them. The Human Readiness Architecture material developed this way.

The canonical published version of each article lives on LinkedIn. The copy here is a timestamped record.

---

## How this folder is arranged

Each article has two files:

- **`.pdf`** — the full record: the post, the accompanying image, the article text, and any substantive public discussion that followed
- **`.md`** — metadata, the frameworks the article touches, and notes on how to read it

The markdown file carries structured frontmatter listing the related frameworks and any emerging themes. This is what makes the collection navigable: an article often touches several frameworks at once, and some touch none that exist yet.

Articles are stored flat, not filed under a framework. Most span more than one, and filing by framework would either misrepresent them or duplicate them.

---

## Emerging themes

Where an article develops a line of thinking that does not yet correspond to a framework in this repository, it is tagged under `emerging_themes` rather than forced into an existing category. Themes accumulating across several articles are the most likely candidates for the next framework.

Current themes being tracked:

- Cross-domain pattern recognition as a governance capability
- Strategic stewardship as a distinct organisational role
