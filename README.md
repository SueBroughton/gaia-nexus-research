# gaia-nexus-research

**Human–AI relational governance, identity, coherence, continuity and human readiness.**

Research archive and evolving framework architecture, documenting work by Sue Broughton on the human side of human–AI systems.

Most work on AI governance measures the machine. This programme measures what happens to the people working alongside it, and to the relationship between them — a half that remains largely unmeasured in practice, and that degrades quietly while technical indicators improve.

## A note on dates

Publication dates establish precedence in this work, and they are stated explicitly in the text of every index and framework README. GitHub commit timestamps record when a file was uploaded here, not when the work was published, and cannot be backdated.

Earlier foundations were published as academic papers during 2025, including *The Fractal Architecture of Consciousness* (Zenodo, 18 December 2025, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17970333](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17970333)).

## Validation status

Nothing in this archive has been empirically validated. These are conceptual architectures developed through sustained longitudinal engagement with AI systems and refined through public dialogue. There are no validated instruments and no controlled longitudinal datasets. Each framework README states plainly what is and is not established in its own area.
