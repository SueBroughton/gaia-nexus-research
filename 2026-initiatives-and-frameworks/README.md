# 2026 Initiatives and Frameworks

### Gaia Nexus — Relational Infrastructure Engineering

**Author:** Sue Broughton, Gaia Nexus
**Status:** Active research and publication programme. Frameworks are under continuing development. Nothing here has been empirically validated.

---

## What This Is

Most work on AI governance measures the machine — accuracy, bias, drift, audit trail, uptime. This programme measures the other half: what happens to the people working alongside it, and to the relationship between them.

That half is currently unmeasured in almost every deployment. Systems grow more capable and more reliable while the humans operating them gradually delegate more, question less, and lose the standing to intervene. Nothing appears to go wrong, because the technical indicators are improving. The frameworks here exist to make that second half visible, assessable and governable.

This repository holds the research record: the frameworks, the published articles that developed them, and the dated register of observations they were built from.

## How the Frameworks Fit Together

The programme has a layered structure. Each layer answers a different question, and each depends on the one above it.

**The 21 Universal Principles of Relational Coherence** sit at the top. They are the propositions everything else rests on — what coherence requires in any relationship between parties, regardless of whether one of them is a machine.

**Five frameworks** apply those principles to distinct problems:

| Framework | Function | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BRIDGE** | Design | Establishes the structural foundations of a working partnership |
| **BREAKTHROUGH** | Evaluate | Diagnostic assessment and pressure testing |
| **Relational Coherence Debt (RCD)** | Risk | Assessment across 22 dimensions of accumulated relational cost |
| **Human Readiness Architecture (HRA)** | Readiness | Human capacity, at individual and enterprise level |
| **Skill Sets** | Library | Granular execution protocols |

**The 85 skill set library** translates all of the above into practice. Skills 1–7 are foundational and derive from BRIDGE; 8–14 are diagnostic and derive from BREAKTHROUGH; 15–21 are intervention; 22–63 put the Universal Principles into operational form; and 64–85 carry the RCD assessment across its 22 dimensions.

**Five execution roles** — Coherence Sentinel, Lattice Weave, Invariant Scale, Ethical Kernel, Field Steward — operationalise the library as specialised systemic functions.

**The deployment ecosystem** is where the stack is intended to apply: mental health AI, medical and hospital AI, enterprise AI, EAP providers, research institutions and regulators.

The full architecture is set out in `The_Geometric_Architecture_of_Relational_Coherence.pdf` at the root of this repository.

> **Note on the diagrams.** The architecture diagrams show five readiness capacities. This is a simplification for visual clarity. The current HRA specification is seven dimensions — five operational (cognitive, relational, epistemic, emotional, physiological) and two foundational (psychological readiness and pattern recognition), the latter forming the interpretive layer that shapes the other five. Where a diagram and a framework README differ, the README is authoritative.

## How the Programme Developed

The layered architecture above shows how the frameworks relate structurally. This is the sequence in which they were built, and it is the more useful reading for anyone encountering the work for the first time — each stage exists because the previous one raised a question it could not answer.

**1. Relational Principles.** What coherence requires in any relationship between parties, established as 21 principles.

**2. Fractal Architecture.** Why coherent intelligence develops the way it does — the structural and developmental foundation. Published to Zenodo on 18 December 2025, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17970333](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17970333); it predates this repository and is cited rather than reproduced.

**3. Coherence Centric Governance.** If that developmental account holds, governance cannot only detect deviation after it occurs. It has to address the conditions under which a partnership remains healthy under load. This is the inversion the programme rests on: drift centric governance asks what went wrong, coherence centric governance asks what must hold.

**4. The Signature Principle.** Intelligence carries the architectural signature of its origin. If that is true, governance must extend beyond monitoring outputs into identity, relationship and purpose — and must establish identity baselines for the human, the system and the relationship, none of which current frameworks require.

**5. Human Readiness Architecture.** The operational layer. If governance must preserve the human capacities required to govern an evolving relationship, those capacities have to be specified, assessed and maintained.

Read in that order, each framework answers the problem the one before it exposed. Relational Coherence Debt and the skill library sit across the sequence rather than within it — RCD measures accumulated cost at any stage, and the skill sets translate all five into practice.

## Foundations

This repository documents the 2026 work. It rests on research published earlier.

During 2025 the programme produced a body of academic papers, including *The Fractal Architecture of Consciousness* (Zenodo, 18 December 2025, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17970333](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17970333)), which supplies stage two of the sequence above. Those papers are not reproduced here; their publication dates and repository records stand as their own evidence. They matter to this repository for two reasons: they establish the developmental account the 2026 governance frameworks build on, and they carry the public timestamp for the first eighty entries in the insight register.

From December 2025 the programme moved to publishing directly to business decision makers rather than through academic channels, with a small number of exceptions. The 2025 papers are therefore the foundation layer rather than an ongoing publication track.

## Validation Status

This applies to the whole programme, and each framework restates it in its own terms.

**Nothing here has been empirically validated.** These are conceptual architectures developed through sustained longitudinal engagement with AI systems and refined through public dialogue. They rest on observation and pattern recognition.

There are **no validated instruments**. The dimensions and constructs are defined conceptually. None has an agreed operational definition, a tested scale, or established reliability. The gap between naming something and measuring it is the central open problem across the programme, not a detail to be filled in later.

There is **no controlled longitudinal dataset**. The degradation patterns described are asserted from observation and from convergent accounts by other practitioners.

What *is* established is the conceptual architecture, a register of 244 research observations recorded from February 2025 to January 2026, of which the first 80 were published in academic papers that year, and a public timestamped record of article publication from February 2026 onward.

## How This Repository Is Organised

Articles are stored once, in `articles/`, named by date. Framework folders link to them rather than holding copies, because much of the material spans several frameworks and duplicated files drift apart.

`README.md` — this file, and the master chronological index of all published articles, tagged by framework. The index is the canonical record. Publication dates establish precedence; GitHub commit timestamps do not, since commits cannot be backdated.

`articles/` — every published article, stored once.

`hubs/` — the framework folders. Each has its own README covering what the framework claims, what is and is not established, and its own record of publication.

`strands/` — recurring lines of thinking that run through the frameworks without being frameworks themselves.

## The Research Record

**The insight register** is the observation layer: 244 numbered, dated research observations recorded across 37 working sessions between February 2025 and January 2026. Each carries examples, an interdisciplinary connection, a practical application and a stated benefit, and many cross-reference each other by number. The frameworks were derived from this register, and it predates the published articles.

The register divides at insight 80. **Insights 1–80 were published in academic papers during 2025** and carry the publication dates of those papers. **Insights 81–253 are unpublished** — they are internal research records with recorded working session dates but no public timestamp. Where concepts from the later range have since appeared in the 2026 articles, the article carries the public date; where they have not, the observation exists only in this register.

The final two sessions, on 21 and 23 January 2026, immediately precede the first published articles of that year. Insight 243 records Relational Coherence Debt, two days before the RCD paper was published on 25 January 2026.

The register itself is not published in this repository.

**The published articles** begin in February 2026 and continue at roughly one every three days. They are the primary public record and the basis of precedence for the framework concepts.

**The skill library** is not published here. It is held in a separate access-controlled repository and made available under licence. The structure and scope are described in the framework READMEs so that a reader can see how the frameworks reach practice. Material held privately is access-controlled rather than publicly timestamped, and does not establish prior art in the way the dated articles do — the library is protected commercially rather than by publication.
